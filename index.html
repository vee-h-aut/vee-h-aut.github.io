Airwise

Find HVAC waste before it shows up on your power bill.

A wireless sensor kit with AI fault detection for small Auckland and Wellington commercial buildings, designed to be plug-and-play and to reduce the need for specialist installation and rewiring.

How Airwise works

From box to insight without a contractor.

1

Stick on the sensors

Wireless sensors track temperature and HVAC activity, designed to self-calibrate with minimal setup.

2

AI watches continuously

Anomaly detection flags faults, overnight running and conflicting heating and cooling in real time.

3

Act on clear alerts

Each alert comes with a plain-English action and an auto-generated, optimised schedule.

Why Airwise

Versus full building management systems

Airwise aims to provide a more accessible monitoring option for small buildings that may not need or be able to afford a comprehensive system.

Versus other monitoring tools

Airwise aims to combine wireless sensors, AI-assisted fault detection and scheduling recommendations in one straightforward platform.

Designed for simpler adoption

Airwise is being developed around plug-and-play installation, with the goal of reducing the need for specialist installation and rewiring.

Focused on actionable insights

Alerts are intended to help building managers identify potential HVAC problems and energy waste, then decide what action to take.

Pricing

$50–100 per storey per month, depending on the scale of the HVAC system. Indicative pricing, to be refined through pilots.
