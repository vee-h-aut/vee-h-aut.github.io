Airwise

Find HVAC waste before it shows up on your power bill.

A wireless sensor kit with AI fault detection for small Auckland and Wellington commercial buildings, designed to be plug-and-play.

How it works

  1. Place the wireless sensors, designed for minimal setup.
  2. AI monitors HVAC performance and flags faults and waste.
  3. Review clear alerts and a suggested schedule, then decide what to do.

Why Airwise

  • A more accessible option than a full building management system.
  • Sensors, AI fault detection and scheduling in one platform.
  • Alerts help you decide what action to take.
